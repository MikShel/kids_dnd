# What to print

Ready-to-print PDFs are in `print/`. If you edit any `.md` file, rebuild them with `./build.sh`.

## Print

| # | File | What it is | Copies | Paper | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `print/персонажи.pdf` | 8 hero sheets (+1 GM note page) | 1 full set | Thick paper (160–200 g), colour | You assign heroes from the home quizzes (see `пробная/`); print all 8 anyway so you can swap. Page 1 is a note for you, skip it if you like. |
| 2 | `print/карточки.pdf` | 15 puzzle/item cards + hearts sheet | 1 set | Thick paper, colour | Cut out. Card 14 is 16 small pictures on one page (cut them apart). Print the potion and medal pages once each, they already hold 3 potions / 4 medals. The hearts page is only needed if you have no buttons or beans. |
| 3 | `print/правила.pdf` | Rules for the kids | 1–2 | Normal, colour | Lay it in the middle of the table. Kids don't need to read it before the game. |
| 4 | `print/сценарий.pdf` | GM script with answers | 1 (for you) | Normal, b/w is fine | Or read it from a tablet. |

**Total:** about 38 pages, 29 of them in colour.

**Optional:**
- The pictures you generate (see `промпты.md`): scene pictures on A4 to show as each scene starts, or on a tablet/TV.
- Hero portraits: print small ones and glue them onto the "Нарисуй своего героя" box (or let the kids draw, they usually love that more).

## Don't print — get these

| Item | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| d20 dice | 1 per kid ideal, at least 2 | Rolling your own die is half the fun. |
| Heart tokens | ~30 | Buttons, beans, glass gems or gummy bears. 5 per hero + 9 for goblins. |
| Pencils & crayons | 1 per kid | For writing names and drawing heroes. |
| "Firefly jar" (optional, very worth it) | 1 | Any jar with a small LED tealight or fairy lights inside. Hand it over in scene 3, kids give it to the goblin chief in scene 6. |
| Scissors | 1 | For the cards. |
| Chocolate coins (optional) | 1 per kid | Reward with the medal at the end. |

## How to print

- Open each PDF in `print/` and print it on A4. Use **"Actual size" / 100%**, not "fit to page".
- Each hero and each card starts on a new page.

## Before the game: trial & home quiz

Starting with a new kid? Do the short trial game and the home quiz first: see `пробная/печать.md` for what to print (`./пробная/build.sh` rebuilds it).
