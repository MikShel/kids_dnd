# Prompts for 2D pictures and video

Prompts are in English because image and video generators follow English best. They work in ChatGPT/DALL·E, Midjourney, Gemini/Imagen, Ideogram, Leonardo and similar tools. The video prompts are for Sora, Veo, Runway, Kling or Pika.

## How to keep everything in one style

1. **Always paste the STYLE block** at the end of every prompt.
2. **Make the wizard first.** When you like it, use it as the style reference for everything else. In Midjourney add `--sref <image URL>`, and for the same character again `--cref <image URL>`. In ChatGPT/Gemini, upload the image and say "same style and same character as this picture".
3. **No text in pictures.** AI garbles Cyrillic letters. If you need words, such as the goblin note or the door, add them afterwards in Canva or Word. The cards already contain the text.
4. **Aspect ratios:** scenes `16:9` (to show on a TV or tablet), heroes and characters `3:4` (to print on the hero sheet), items and tokens `1:1` on a white background.

### STYLE block (copy into every prompt)

```
2D children's storybook illustration, flat colours with soft gouache texture, clean thick outlines, warm cheerful palette, cute rounded characters with big friendly eyes, soft lighting, cozy fairy-tale mood, nothing scary, suitable for 6-year-old children, no text, no letters, no words
```

## 5. Short videos (2D animation, 5–10 seconds)

Best method: **generate the scene picture first, then use image-to-video** with these motion prompts. This keeps the style consistent. Add this line to every video prompt:

```
2D hand-drawn animation, children's cartoon style, smooth gentle motion, cheerful, no text
```

**Intro (start of the game)**
```
The camera slowly flies over a cozy fairy-tale valley at sunrise toward a tall wizard's tower; inside a round window a rainbow crystal glows on a cushion; then the light flickers and the crystal is gone, leaving only sparkles. [VIDEO STYLE]
```

**The theft (show after scene 1)**
```
At night, three funny green goblins (two small, one big with a bottle-cap crown) tiptoe through a wizard's room, the big one grabs a glowing rainbow crystal and they sneak out of the window, giggling, leaving muddy footprints. [VIDEO STYLE]
```

**Gnome Hans says hello (scene 3)**
```
A small gnome with a red cap sitting on a mossy stone by a river waves hello with a big smile, his beard bouncing, the jar of fireflies on his belt glowing and twinkling. [VIDEO STYLE]
```

**The magic door opens (scene 5)**
```
A round stone door in a cave: four symbols (moon, star, sun, flame) light up one after another, then the door rumbles and rolls aside, golden light pouring out, sleeping bats wake up and flutter away. [VIDEO STYLE]
```

**Burchun gets his fireflies (scene 6)**
```
A grumpy green goblin chief on a crate throne receives a glowing jar of fireflies, his eyes grow wide and he slowly smiles, fireflies light up the dark cave, he hands over a rainbow crystal. [VIDEO STYLE]
```

**Victory — lantern festival (scene 7)**
```
A rainbow crystal is placed on top of a wizard's tower and a wave of light spreads across a village square, a hundred colourful lanterns light up one by one, fireworks burst in the evening sky, a gnome and a goblin dance together. [VIDEO STYLE]
```
