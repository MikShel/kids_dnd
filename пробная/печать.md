# What to print: trial game and home quiz

Ready-to-print PDFs are in `print/`. If you edit any `.md` file, rebuild them with `./build.sh` (run it from this folder or as `./пробная/build.sh` from the project root).

## Print

| # | File | What it is | Copies | Paper | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `print/опросник.pdf` | Home quiz: which hero suits your child | 1 per child | Normal, b/w is fine | Read the questions aloud, the child answers. Do it before or after the trial game, any time before the big game. |
| 2 | `print/родителям.pdf` | Short guide for parents | 1 per child | Normal, b/w is fine | Read it once before the game. |
| 3 | `print/карточки.pdf` | Trial room cards (chest, cabinet, parrot, items, badge, Kesha's 16 pictures, lucky feather) | 1 set; plus the П11, П12 and П14 pages once per extra child | Thick paper (160–200 g), colour | Cut out. Each card is on its own page. Page 1 is a note for you. П11 (items) and П12 (badge) are cut out and given away, so print them again for each extra child. The badge is the reward, write the child's name on it. П13 is 16 small pictures on one page: cut them apart, they are reusable. П14 (lucky feather) is given away. |
| 4 | `print/герой.pdf` | Trial hero sheet | 1 per child | Thick paper, colour | Page 1 is the questions for you to read aloud, page 2 is the hero sheet. |
| 5 | `print/сценарий.pdf` | GM script with answers | 1 (for you) | Normal, b/w is fine | Or read it from a tablet. |

## Don't print — get these

| Item | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| d20 dice | 1 per kid ideal, at least 1 | Rolling your own die is half the fun. |
| Heart tokens | 5 per hero | Buttons, beans, glass gems or gummy bears. |
| Pencils & crayons | 1 per kid | For writing the name and drawing the hero. |
| Scissors | 1 | For the cards. |

## How to print

- Open each PDF in `print/` and print it on A4. Use **"Actual size" / 100%**, not "fit to page".
- Each card starts on a new page.
